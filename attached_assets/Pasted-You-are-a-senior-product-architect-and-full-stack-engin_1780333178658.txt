You are a senior product architect and full-stack engineer. I want to build 
a privacy-focused social network called **"Quietude"** (or suggest a better name).

---

## CONCEPT

A social space where people share thoughts, writing, and media — but with 
zero algorithmic manipulation. No engagement metrics. No comparisons. 
No anxiety loops. Just calm, intentional connection.

Core philosophy:
- Chronological feed only — no ranking, no boosting
- No public like counts, follower counts, or view counts
- No "trending" or "viral" mechanics
- No data selling, no ad targeting
- Posts disappear after 30 days by default (optional permanence)
- Anonymous-first: display names optional, real names never required

---

## WHAT I NEED FROM YOU

### 1. Product Design Document
- Define the core user flows (sign up → post → connect → read)
- List features for MVP vs V2
- Identify what you intentionally *remove* from typical social networks 
  and *why* it matters for mental health

### 2. Tech Stack Recommendation
- Recommend a privacy-first full-stack architecture
- Consider: end-to-end encryption for DMs, self-hostable option, 
  GDPR/data minimization, no third-party analytics
- Should be buildable by a solo dev or small team (MERN or similar)
- Explain trade-offs of your choices

### 3. Database Schema (MongoDB or PostgreSQL)
- Users, Posts, Connections, DMs
- Ephemeral post logic (TTL / scheduled deletion)
- Privacy settings per-post and per-user

### 4. API Design
- RESTful or GraphQL — justify your choice
- Core endpoints: auth, posts CRUD, feed (chronological), 
  follow/connect, DM, delete account (full data wipe)
- Include rate limiting and abuse prevention strategy

### 5. Frontend Architecture
- React-based, component breakdown
- UI philosophy: calm, minimal, no red notification badges, 
  soft interactions, no dark patterns
- Suggest a design system / color language that reduces anxiety 
  (think: warm neutrals, generous whitespace, no infinite scroll)

### 6. Privacy & Security Checklist
- Auth strategy (JWT + refresh tokens / sessions)
- Data minimization practices
- How to handle account deletion thoroughly
- How to prevent scraping and bot abuse without invasive CAPTCHAs

### 7. Monetization Without Surveillance
- Suggest 2–3 ethical revenue models (e.g., optional subscriptions, 
  one-time purchases, donations)
- What to explicitly *never* do (and why)
